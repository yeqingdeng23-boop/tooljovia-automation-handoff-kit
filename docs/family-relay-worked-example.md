# Family Relay: a completely fictional example

Everything below is invented for this worksheet. It is not a customer's story or a record of a real family.

## 1. Choose a beginning

- **Story title:** The Lantern That Took Its Time
- **Invented character name and what they want:** Pip, a paper fox who wants to return a lost lantern.
- **Another invented character or companion:** Mallow, a moth with a tiny map.
- **Imaginary place:** The Clockwork Meadow, where the grass ticks softly.
- **Opening sentence:** On a slow Tuesday, Pip found a lantern glowing beneath a blue mushroom.

## 2. Pass the story along

**Turn 1 — What happens first?** Pip lifted the lantern and found a paper tag that read “For the cloud keeper.” Mallow offered to lead the way across the meadow.

**Turn 2 — What changes or surprises the characters?** Halfway across, the lantern went dark. They listened to the ticking grass and discovered it glowed again whenever they walked slowly.

**Turn 3 — How does this little adventure end?** Pip and Mallow reached the hill at an easy pace and returned the lantern to the cloud keeper. In thanks, the keeper hung one small star above their path home.

## 3. Make and keep the book

The storytellers could put their invented lines into [Our Story](https://tooljovia.com/our-story/) with a grown-up. After editing, they would keep an editable story parcel private and save an illustrated HTML book for reading. A cover PNG could be shared separately after checking the title and image; it would not include the editable book.

## 4. Before sharing

- [x] Names and places are invented; no identifying details are included.
- [x] The imaginary storytellers agree that the cover may be shared.
- [x] The cover would use an illustration they have permission to share.
- [x] They understand the parcel and reading HTML may contain the story, while the PNG is only a cover.
- [x] A grown-up would keep the editable parcel private.

**Choice:** Share cover only; keep the editable parcel and HTML book private.

**One thing to check before finishing:** Make sure the cover title does not contain a real person's name.
