# Family Relay: make a story together

Use this worksheet with a grown-up to invent a story in turns. It is a starting place, not a form you need to submit. Copy it into a private document, print it, or write the answers on paper. See the [completed fictional example](family-relay-worked-example.md) for one way to fill it in.

Use pretend names and invented details. Do not include a child's real name, photos, address, identifying details, private family stories or beliefs. An adult can write down a younger storyteller's words.

## 3. Make and keep the book

1. A grown-up can open [Our Story by ToolJovia](https://tooljovia.com/our-story/), choose **Enter our little world**, then **Family Relay**, then **Let’s make a little book**. It is free, and no account is needed to create. Story work stays in that browser; this worksheet is separate from the site.
2. Enter your **Opening sentence** in **How does your story begin?**, your invented character in **Who will we follow?**, and your imaginary place in **Where does the first page open?** Select **Continue**, choose a title and a little world, then **Continue** and **Make our little book**.
3. Choose **Open our book**. Use **Rewrite this page** to shape the first turn, then **Save our page**. Use **Add the next little page** for each later turn, enter the words and select **Save our page**. If you add a picture, use one you made or have permission to use.
4. Return to **My book’s room**. Choose **Keep this little book** and then **Download editable story parcel** or **Download illustrated book**. To make a cover picture, choose **Make a family story parcel**, then **Preview cover card** and **Save PNG**. Decide what to keep or share; these are different files:
   - **Story parcel:** an editable copy containing the book's pages, drawings and bylines. Keep it private; a grown-up may manually pass the downloaded file privately to someone they know using a trusted service. The recipient opens a new copy in Our Story, and each browser keeps its own independent version. After a turn, download and pass the updated parcel back yourself; the website does not send or synchronize it.
   - **Illustrated HTML book:** a reading keepsake with words and pictures. It is not the editable story parcel. Save it privately and open it in a browser to read.
   - **Cover PNG:** one shareable picture with a title. It is not the full book or an editable copy. The address printed on it is not a clickable book link.

Browser storage can be cleared or lost, so keep private downloaded copies if you want to return to the story.
