# Read a finished story in Once, My Way

From the ToolJovia team: explore a fictional example before creating your own book.

1. Open [Once, My Way](https://tooljovia.com/once-my-way/) and choose **See a finished example**. The example is read-only and opening it does not save or download a book.
2. Read **The Ugly Duckling**: five story pages follow four recorded choices and end with **A Home for Every Feather**. The first page begins: “The last egg in the nest opened with a determined crack.” This is an imagined example, not a family's submission.
3. Choose **Return without changing my page** to return, then select an adapted classic and explore your own choices. Edit a title or sentence and keep your version on the local bookshelf. Alternatively, **Save a copy** explicitly saves the fictional example to your browser so you can make it your own.

The adaptation is based on Hans Christian Andersen, with AI-assisted writing and AI-generated illustrations. The tool offers an illustrated HTML download of the chosen path; the finished download records that path and does not add branching controls. Check your saved file on the device where you intend to read it. No account or email is needed for the free tool.
