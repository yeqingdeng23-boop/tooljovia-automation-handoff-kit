# A five-minute read-aloud: Cinderella

A simple activity for a grown-up and child, from the ToolJovia team.

**Before you start:** Open [Once, My Way](https://tooljovia.com/once-my-way/) and choose **Cinderella**. A grown-up should look through the story first and decide whether it suits the child. The activity can be shortened or extended.

## Read and choose together

1. **Start the story.** Read the invitation scene aloud. Cinderella can arrange a fair division of chores first or prepare for the magical journey. Ask: “What might each choice change for Cinderella?” Choose together.
2. **At the second choice**, pause and ask: “Who might be affected by this choice? What do you think could happen next?” Pick a path and continue reading.
3. **At the third choice**, ask: “What does Cinderella seem to want now? Which path would you like to explore, and why?” Choose together.
4. **At the fourth choice**, ask: “Which ending would you like to discover? Is there another way the story could end?” Make the final choice and read the ending together.

## Make it your own

If you wish, use the story's writing option to change a sentence in your own version. You can also return to a choice and explore another path. No answer needs to be shared with us.
