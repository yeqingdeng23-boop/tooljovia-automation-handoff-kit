# Make a free pet photo keepsake card

One photograph and a few familiar words can make a small keepsake to save for yourself. This guide uses [Find Your Pet's Soul](https://tooljovia.com/pet-soul/), a ToolJovia tool. We work on ToolJovia, so this is a guide to our own product. The photo card and text download described here are free; no account or purchase is needed.

You can use a photo you took yourself or another image you have permission to use. If you are just trying the steps, use an invented name and an ordinary sentence. A photograph is optional, and you do not need to write about a loss, choose a faith, or describe a dream.

## 1. Choose a photo and a few words

Open [Find Your Pet's Soul](https://tooljovia.com/pet-soul/) in your browser. Under **A photo of your companion (optional)**, choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP file from your device. The limit is 8 MiB and 24 megapixels. The photo is decoded locally on your device; it is not uploaded for the card.

The name field, **Your companion's name (optional)**, accepts up to 40 characters. In **Their story and your memories (optional)**, you can write up to 3,000 characters. A simple detail is enough: perhaps a favourite place to nap or a funny everyday habit. If you prefer not to add a name or story, leave those fields blank.

For a fictional practice run, you might use the name “Maple” and the sentence “Maple always chose the sunny corner.” These details are invented, not a customer's story. Use your own authorised image, or start without one.

## 2. Make the free reflection and find the card

Select **Create my free soul-journey reflection ✧**. The page creates the free local reflection and shows a section called **Give a memory a photograph**. This is where you can review the image card. Your photo remains on your device in this card workflow.

The card uses a short excerpt rather than the whole text. This makes the card easy to read, but it also means you should check the preview before saving. If you wrote a longer memory and want to keep all the text, use the separate text download described below.

## 3. Frame the picture and choose the words

Under **Photo framing**, select **Fill the frame (may crop)** if you want the picture to fill the card. Use **Move the crop vertically** to adjust the visible part of the image. Select **Keep the whole photo** when you want to avoid cropping. Compare the preview with your original so you do not accidentally cut out something important.

Under **Text source for your card**, choose **Original story** to use words from the story you entered, or **Current memorial letter** to use the letter as it currently appears in **Words to keep**. You can edit that letter first. Then use **Choose an excerpt from your story** to pick the short passage for the card and review how it looks. If you change the letter, check the card preview again before saving.

## 4. Save the card and, if useful, the full text

When the preview looks right, select **Save memorial image .png**. Check your browser's downloads and open the PNG on your device. Keep your original photograph too; a card is a separate file. If a browser asks where to save the file, choose a location you can find again.

For the text, select **Preview the exact text, including any faith information** and read what will be included. If you want a separate text file, select **Download text .txt**. Check and open that file too. A downloaded file remains on your device even if you later clear the page, so remove it separately if you no longer want to keep it.

## Privacy and the optional work

The free card is made locally and does not require you to send us your photo or post your memories. You can leave optional fields empty. The separate free chapter preview and optional personal reflection work involve a different step: the page asks for your explicit choice before sending text for that preview. The photo is not sent with it. Read the information on the page before choosing what to submit.

The site also offers an optional personal reflection work for a one-time USD 59.90. You can use the free card and text download without buying it. The work is a personal reflection, not evidence of a pet's location, reincarnation, or a verified message. A [complete example](https://tooljovia.com/pet-soul/examples/juniper-full-booklet) is clearly labelled **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION**; its details are invented.

Keep the card private or share it only when you are comfortable. There is no need to send your photo, story, beliefs, or dreams to ToolJovia as feedback.
