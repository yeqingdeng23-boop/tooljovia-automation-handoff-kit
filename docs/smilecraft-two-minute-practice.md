# Your two-minute SmileCraft practice

A small rehearsal before a photo or video call can help you explore an expression that feels comfortable. SmileCraft's English browser preview is free, with no account or payment. Use an ordinary mirror, the optional camera mirror, or no mirror at all.

## 1. Open the workspace

[Open the two-minute practice](https://tooljovia-smilecraft-preview.pages.dev/train/mimic/).

You can begin without a video or camera. This preview does not include a public real-person video library. A neutral or closed-mouth expression is a valid choice, too.

## 2. Choose your mirror

For an ordinary mirror, simply keep the camera off.

For the optional on-device mirror, choose your **Age range**, check **I want a local, unrecorded mirror. No analysis or uploads.**, then select **Enable mirror choice** and **Start local mirror**. Allow the browser's camera request only if you want it. The camera mirror is for people 13 and older.

SmileCraft does not record this mirror, request your microphone or calculate a score here. Enabling the mirror does not authorize the separate camera-analysis feature. If permission is denied, continue without a camera.

## 3. Optionally add your own video

Under **Use your own reference**, confirm that you have permission to use the real-person video, then select **Choose video**.

Use a supported MP4 or WebM, 2–60 seconds long and up to 25 MB. If it cannot open, try an H.264 MP4 or another supported WebM. No video is needed to finish the practice.

Your file plays locally; it is not uploaded, added to a public library or analyzed. Select **Play example**. The **Playback speed** control offers **Half speed**; **Loop this clip**, **Hold this frame** and **Video position** let you repeat, pause or seek. Video playback and the practice timer have separate controls.

## 4. Start the two minutes

Select **Start two-minute practice**. Follow the page's cues: observe a small movement, try it gently, let your eyes move naturally, then release. Keep your jaw comfortable and stop any movement that feels uncomfortable.

Use **Pause practice**, **Resume practice** or **Reset practice** as needed. Switching tabs or hiding this page pauses the timer and video and stops the camera mirror. Returning does not restart them automatically; resume the controls you want to use.

## 5. Finish with one useful observation

At **Your practice is complete.**, the mirror stops and the workspace removes your selected video. Your original file remains unchanged on your device. Choose it again for another session.

The completion record stays in this tab and disappears on refresh. This is an unscored rehearsal, not a measurement of beauty, emotion or similarity to another person. Decide whether there is one comfortable cue you want to try at your next photo or call.

For a simpler text-led option, [open Practice](https://tooljovia-smilecraft-preview.pages.dev/train/).
