# Your two-minute SmileCraft practice

A small rehearsal before a photo or video call can help you explore an expression that feels comfortable. SmileCraft's English browser preview is free, with no account or payment. Use an ordinary mirror, the optional camera mirror, or no mirror at all.

## Free text routines

Choose [A softer start](https://tooljovia-smilecraft-preview.pages.dev/train/mirror/), [Move with ease](https://tooljovia-smilecraft-preview.pages.dev/train/movement/) or [Ready for the moment](https://tooljovia-smilecraft-preview.pages.dev/train/situation/). All three are free, text-led and camera-free.

1. Select **Start practice** and follow one gentle cue at a time for two minutes. Stop if a movement is uncomfortable.
2. Use **Pause** when needed. Leaving or hiding the page also pauses it. Return and select **Continue this practice** when you are ready; it never continues automatically. Starting a different routine replaces the paused one.
3. Without saving, paused progress stays only during navigation in this app tab. A refresh, tab closure or full-page navigation loses it. To choose on-device saving, open [Local storage choices](https://tooljovia-smilecraft-preview.pages.dev/account/) and review the saving checkbox in the **Local history** section. A saved paused routine still waits for **Continue this practice**. Leave saving off on a shared device. Turning it off clears saved history and paused text progress; reload older open SmileCraft tabs after an update. These controls are not instant synchronization or an encrypted account backup.

### Optional: keep a text practice card

Before finishing, select **Preview an example card**. It is labeled **EXAMPLE — NOT A COMPLETED PRACTICE**; making or saving an example does not complete a routine or add a check-in.

After this routine actually finishes, select **Make a practice card**. An earlier check-in or a page reload does not turn an example into a completed card.

Under **Reminder on the card**, choose one cue from that routine or **No reminder**. Wait for the image to appear, then review it. Use **Update PNG preview** if you want to prepare it again. Only after a preview is ready, select **Save example PNG** or **Save PNG** as appropriate. Your browser handles the download; previewing does not automatically save, share or send anything. If preparation fails, follow the message and try again.

The card contains the practice name, optional cue, example/completed status, SmileCraft branding and public entry link. It does not include your age, goals, photos, measurements, scores or history. **Close preview** discards the temporary preview. **Reset** (or **Start over** for a paused routine) also clears the old preview and returns the timer to two minutes without starting it. Reset does not delete earlier check-ins or files you already downloaded; those files remain under your browser/device's control.

## Optional: compare your own two camera samples

[Open the local calibration prototype](https://tooljovia-smilecraft-preview.pages.dev/analyze/). This separate camera flow is optional; it does not use a selected video file or start the two-minute text timer. This is an experimental preview; end-to-end camera, model and physical-device behavior has not yet been validated.

1. Review the two initially unchecked choices: **I am 13 or older.** and local analysis in this tab. Choose them only if they apply and you want to continue; both are needed before Start is available.
2. Face a soft light with one face in view and select **Start my relaxed sample**. Use your own relaxed expression. A successful sample uses six clear seconds after a short settling interval. Allow extra time for model/camera setup; failed attempts need a fresh start, not just more waiting.
3. After the first sample succeeds, the camera stops. Keep the same light, distance and forward-facing position, then separately select **Start my gentle-smile sample**. Use a small, comfortable movement. There is no automatic second sample or resume. If a quality check fails, follow the message and restart from the first sample.
4. Use **Stop and clear calibration** at any time, or **Clear results and start again** after comparison. Hiding or leaving the page, or withdrawing either choice, clears both samples. Results stay in page memory and do not enter SmileCraft history; there is no recording or upload.

The table compares sample medians of average corner height relative to the lip center, left–right corner-height gap, and lip opening—each divided by mouth width. **Difference** means gentle-smile minus relaxed. Positive and negative values are neither better nor worse. You choose the expression labels; this does not recognize emotions, verify a neutral expression or identity, or demonstrate scientific improvement. Each capture has a separate clock: this is not synchronized motion or reaction time.

No camera, unsupported browser or an uncomfortable movement? Stop and [continue with a free text routine](https://tooljovia-smilecraft-preview.pages.dev/train/).

## Optional mirror and video workspace

The steps below describe a separate workspace. Its timer, video and analysis controls are different from the three text routines above; the practice-card controls belong to the text routines.

## 1. Open the workspace

[Open the two-minute practice](https://tooljovia-smilecraft-preview.pages.dev/train/mimic/).

You can begin without a video or camera. This preview does not include a public real-person video library. A neutral or closed-mouth expression is a valid choice, too.

## 2. Choose your mirror

For an ordinary mirror, simply keep the camera off.

For the optional on-device mirror, choose your **Age range**, check **I want a local, unrecorded mirror. No analysis or uploads.**, then select **Enable mirror choice** and **Start local mirror**. Allow the browser's camera request only if you want it. The camera mirror is for people 13 and older.

SmileCraft does not record this mirror, request your microphone or calculate a score here. Enabling the mirror does not authorize the separate camera-analysis feature. If permission is denied, continue without a camera.

## 3. Optionally add your own video

Under **Use your own reference**, confirm that you have permission to use the real-person video, then select **Choose video**.

Use a supported MP4 or WebM, 2–60 seconds long and up to 25 MB. If it cannot open, try an H.264 MP4 or another supported WebM. No video is needed to finish the practice.

With playback-only permission, your file plays locally; it is not uploaded, added to a public library or analyzed. Playing it does not authorize analysis. Select **Play example**. The **Playback speed** control offers **Half speed**; **Loop this clip**, **Hold this frame** and **Video position** let you repeat, pause or seek. Video playback and the practice timer have separate controls.

### Optional: compare local mouth geometry

For ages 13+; not needed for free practice.

1. Choose **Age range for local geometry checks**, then separately check video-analysis permission.
2. Choose a clear, continuous, forward-facing 5–20-second interval and select **Analyze selected video interval**. Follow any quality error; a reference exists only if checks pass.
3. If wanted, separately check camera-analysis permission and select **Start six-second camera sample**.
4. Results show corner height, left–right corner gap and lip opening as mouth-width ratios, plus camera-minus-video differences—not beauty, emotion, identity, medical conclusions or synchronized-motion similarity percentages.

Images and results stay in this page's working memory: no recording, microphone, uploads or automatic baseline-history entries. Select **Stop and clear local session**, or **Finish and clear this session** after comparison. These buttons also reset the practice timer. Once you opt into analysis, hiding or leaving clears that session and selected video and resets the timer. Hiding an untouched playback-only video pauses it without removing its selection.

## 4. Start the two minutes

Select **Start two-minute practice**. Follow the page's cues: observe a small movement, try it gently, let your eyes move naturally, then release. Keep your jaw comfortable and stop any movement that feels uncomfortable.

Use **Pause practice**, **Resume practice** or **Reset practice** as needed. In playback-only practice, switching tabs or hiding this page pauses the timer and video and stops the camera mirror. Returning does not restart them automatically; resume the controls you want to use.

## 5. Finish with one useful observation

At **Your practice is complete.**, the mirror stops and the workspace removes your selected video. Your original file remains unchanged on your device. Choose it again for another session.

The completion record stays in this tab and disappears on refresh. This is an unscored rehearsal, not a measurement of beauty, emotion or similarity to another person. Decide whether there is one comfortable cue you want to try at your next photo or call.

For a simpler text-led option, [open Practice](https://tooljovia-smilecraft-preview.pages.dev/train/).
