# Find Your Pet's Soul: free example in Telegram

ToolJovia's Telegram bot is called **Pet Soul Journey by ToolJovia**. Its free experience includes a prewritten fictional English example featuring Juniper; it is not your personal result.

## Open the example

1. Open [@ToolJoviaPetSoulBot](https://t.me/ToolJoviaPetSoulBot) in Telegram. A Telegram account is required.
2. Send `/start` and choose **Free experience**.
3. Open the web app when Telegram asks, then choose **See the free local example**.
4. Read the example and its notes at your own pace.

You can view this fixed example without uploading a photo or sharing your own story or beliefs. Your form inputs and photo are not used to generate this prewritten example. Please do not send private stories or photographs in the bot chat.

You can also visit [Find Your Pet's Soul on the web](https://tooljovia.com/pet-soul/).
