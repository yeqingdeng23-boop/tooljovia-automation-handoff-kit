# Worked service proposal: photos and receipts

Read a complete, editable proposal for a **fictional** consulting setup package: a two-week pilot design for six field technicians and one coordinator to organize photos, enter receipt information manually and prepare a weekly handover.

[Read the proposal](ai-freelance-proposal.md) · [Raw Markdown](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yeqingdeng23-boop/tooljovia-automation-handoff-kit/main/examples/service-proposal/ai-freelance-proposal.md) · [Try the proposal tool](https://tooljovia.com/tools/ai-freelance-proposal-builder/)

The file includes proposed deliverables, exclusions, review conditions, a schedule, one bounded revision round and a manual handover. It is separate from the [19-file automation handoff kit](../../kit/).

## Understand the figures

| Item | Fictional assumption |
|---|---|
| One setup package | USD 1,200 |
| Estimated effort | 20 hours |
| Client billing rate | USD 60/hour |
| Valued owner labor cost | USD 40/hour |
| Proposed kickoff / balance | USD 480 / USD 720 |

The labor cost is not the client billing rate. Other cost assumptions are disclosed in the proposal; taxes, acquisition, refunds and unentered costs are excluded. The figures are an exercise, not a market-price recommendation, a current offer, an accepted client quote or ToolJovia profit.

The cost assumptions are included for internal planning education; review and choose what belongs in any client-facing version yourself.

The proposed templates cover up to four jobs, 40 photos and eight receipts per week. These are template-design limits, not a recurring processing service. No templates, training or client acceptance have been delivered by this example.

## Adapt it to one task

1. Review the assumptions in the [pricing calculator](https://tooljovia.com/tools/ai-service-pricing-calculator/), including delivery time and costs.
2. Use the [proposal builder](https://tooljovia.com/tools/ai-freelance-proposal-builder/) to draft your own scope. Transfer figures manually, keeping labor cost and client billing rate separate.
3. Review the deliverables, exclusions, dependencies, revision limit and acceptance conditions. Editing the proposal does not recalculate its figures or synchronize the tools.
4. Copy or download the reviewed draft and use a separately approved manual channel if you decide to share it. The tools do not send proposals, deliver consulting work, sign agreements or take payment.

Keep private client records and credentials out of public examples and feedback. If a specific part of your current task is missing, you can use the [optional public task form](https://github.com/yeqingdeng23-boop/tooljovia-automation-handoff-kit/issues/new?template=current-task.yml); GitHub sign-in is required for an issue.
