# Project proposal - Two-week photo and receipt workflow setup

Prepared for: Example Field Services (fictional)
Scope unit: one consulting setup package. Currency: USD.

FICTIONAL REVIEW EXERCISE ONLY. This is an operator-reviewed proposal draft, not an accepted client project. No customer, payment, completed workflow template, training session, walkthrough or client sign-off is represented.

## Objective and scope
Define a human-reviewed process for six field technicians and one coordinator to name and index photos, manually enter receipt information, prepare a weekly summary and hand over reviewed records.
The setup package covers a two-week pilot design. Template requirements are up to 4 jobs, 40 photos and 8 receipts per week. These quantities define the proposed templates; they are not a recurring document-processing service or proof that any files have been processed.

## Proposed consulting deliverables
- One current/future workflow map and responsibility list.
- One filename/index checklist and one editable receipt-index template.
- One weekly summary template and one human handover checklist.
- One 60-minute human training session with written operating notes.
- One consolidated in-scope revision round as defined below.
The consultant would create these materials separately after approval. ToolJovia has generated only this proposal draft, not the templates or training.

## Acceptance conditions to agree before work
- The workflow map identifies the field technician, reviewer and coordinator responsibilities.
- Each agreed template is editable and includes the approved job reference, filename/index and manual receipt-entry fields.
- In a future client-authorized walkthrough, the coordinator can manually trace one sample job through naming, indexing, receipt entry, weekly summary and handover. All recognition and checks remain human work.
- The coordinator reviews the deliverable/version checklist, returns one consolidated in-scope response and records acceptance only after review. No such walkthrough or acceptance has occurred in this fictional exercise.

## Schedule and dependencies
Draft delivery is proposed within 10 working days after written scope approval and authorized sample access. The coordinator returns one consolidated response within 5 working days. Delivery depends on timely access, source permissions and consolidated feedback; confirm the working calendar and dates before agreement.
